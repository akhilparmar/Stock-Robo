Rules

  • Signals come from Nifty candles. The order is one at-the-money weekly call or put.
  • Buy only. The later sell closes that same contract. A short option is never opened.
  • One lot, and only if the premium times the lot size is within ₹20,000.
  • Call: above VWAP, both EMAs up, and a break of the opening range or CPR top. Put is the mirror. ADX must be at least 22.
  • Many trades allowed, but only one open option at a time (max ₹20,000 debit).
  • Entries 9:30–11:30 and 13:15–14:45. Index stop/target adapt to ATR + ADX with smaller targets: about 0.5R developing, 0.75R trending, 1R strong.
  • Premium trail: first green tick locks breakeven; at +1% premium that floor is locked; stop then trails 1% under the peak. Hard take-profit at +8% premium. The stop never loosens.
  • Supertrend flip, 15:15 flat, and a −35% premium emergency guard (only before the first lock) remain active.
  • Paper auto turns on by itself at market open (09:15). Stop only if you want to pause for today.
