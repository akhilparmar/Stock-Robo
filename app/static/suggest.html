How it picks

  1. Fetch NSE sectoral indices (heatmap).
  2. Take the lowest and highest sectors.
  3. Keep only stocks that have NSE FO options (Neo scrip master).
  4. Lowest: optionable stocks down 0% to −3% → top 3 losers.
  5. Highest: optionable stocks up 0% to +3% → top 3 winners.
  6. If the extreme sector has no FO names in band (e.g. Media), try the next sectors.
  7. Daily TA picks one best loser and one best winner.
