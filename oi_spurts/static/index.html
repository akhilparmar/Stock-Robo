Rules

  • Top 5 Nifty 200 gainers and top 5 losers, by percent change.
  • The stock must also be in the top 20 names on NSE OI spurts, ranked by percent change in open interest.
  • Gainer: open above yesterday’s close, and the 9:15–9:20 candle green → ATM call. Loser: open below yesterday’s close, and that candle red → ATM put.
  • TA gates (same idea as the stock-option suggest page, on 5‑min bars): EMA9/21/50 stack, SMA20, RSI band, MACD histogram side, candle body ≥45%, volume ≥0.7×, and minimum OI %.
  • Passed names are scored. Only the top 2 scores are paper-bought.
  • Stock stop is the tighter of candle-open and 1.25% from entry. Target starts 1:1, then trails.
  • Extra exits: option premium −25%, and time-cut at 11:00 if the stock is still underwater. Flat at 15:15.
  • Auto buys only from 9:20 to 9:24 IST.
